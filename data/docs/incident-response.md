# Incident Response

## Severity levels
SEV1 is a customer-facing outage and requires updates every 30 minutes. SEV2 is degraded service with hourly updates. SEV3 has no customer impact and is handled as a ticket.

## Paging
The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes. If there is no acknowledgement after 15 minutes the secondary on-call is paged.

## Postmortems
Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident requires a blameless postmortem within five business days, including a timeline, root cause and action items with owners.
